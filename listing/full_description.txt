🤖 Ollama AI Chat — Your Personal AI Assistant on Android

Chat with powerful AI models on your own terms: connect to your Ollama server, or run models fully on-device with no server and no internet required. Beautiful, private, and fast.

✨ TWO WAYS TO CHAT

🌐 Remote (Ollama server)
Connect to any Ollama server on your network and chat with Llama, Mistral, Qwen, DeepSeek and more — with responses streamed in real time.

📲 On-device (Google LiteRT-LM)
Download and run models directly on your phone — Gemma 4, Gemma 3, Qwen 3, DeepSeek R1 Distill and Phi-4 Mini. No server, no network, nothing leaves your device.

✨ KEY FEATURES

💬 Real-time streaming chat in a clean Material Design 3 interface
⏹️ Stop generation mid-reply — keep whatever was produced so far
🎚️ Per-chat model parameters: temperature, top-p, top-k, context length (num_ctx) and seed
📊 Token-speed stats: tokens/sec, token counts and response time under each reply
🧠 Thinking models: see the reasoning from models like DeepSeek R1
🖼️ Image support for vision-capable models, with smart compression
🎙️ Voice input (speech-to-text) and read-aloud replies (text-to-speech)
ℹ️ Model details: inspect parameters, quantization, template, license and modelfile
🏷️ Organize chats with labels and filter the thread list by them
📌 Pin and archive threads to keep things tidy
🔎 Search within a chat, or 🌐 search across every conversation at once
📤 Export and share chats — or single messages — as Markdown
✏️ Per-message actions: copy, share, edit-and-resend, regenerate, delete
📚 Persistent local history with a secure on-device database
🎨 Full dark theme and Material You dynamic colors
📱 Home-screen widget and a "resume last chat" launcher shortcut

🔒 PRIVACY-FIRST
Your conversations stay on your device. With on-device models, nothing ever leaves your phone. With a remote server, you decide exactly where your data goes.

🔧 PERFECT FOR
• Developers testing and comparing AI models
• Researchers experimenting with LLMs
• Anyone who wants private, offline-capable AI on their phone

💡 GETTING STARTED
• On-device: open the app, add the on-device backend, download a model, and start chatting — no server setup required.
• Remote: install Ollama on your computer (ollama.ai), then enter your server URL (e.g. http://192.168.1.100:11434) in the app.

📋 REQUIREMENTS
• Android 7.0 (API 24) or higher
• For remote chat: an accessible Ollama server
• For on-device chat: free storage for the model you choose (≈300 MB–4 GB)
