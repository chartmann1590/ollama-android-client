Ollama AI Chat - private AI on Android

Chat with powerful AI models on your own terms. Connect to your own Ollama server, or run supported models fully on-device with LiteRT-LM. Your conversations are stored locally on your device.

Two ways to chat

Remote Ollama
Connect to any Ollama server on your network and chat with Llama, Mistral, Qwen, DeepSeek, and more, with responses streamed in real time.

On-device models
Download and run supported models directly on your phone. No server is required, and on-device model conversations do not need an internet connection after setup.

Key features

- Real-time streaming chat in a clean Material Design 3 interface
- Stop generation mid-reply and keep the partial text
- Per-chat model parameters: temperature, top-p, top-k, context length, and seed
- Token-speed stats under assistant replies
- Thinking-model support for reasoning traces where available
- Image support for vision-capable models with smart compression
- Voice input and read-aloud replies
- On-device menu and error-message translation with language selection during onboarding and in Settings
- Model details: inspect parameters, quantization, template, license, and modelfile
- Organize chats with labels, pins, and archives
- Search within a chat or across every conversation
- Export and share chats or single messages as Markdown
- Per-message actions: copy, share, edit and resend, regenerate, delete, and report
- Persistent local history with a secure on-device database
- Dark theme and Material You dynamic colors
- Home-screen widget and launcher shortcuts

Privacy first

Your conversations stay on your device unless you choose to connect to a remote server. With on-device models, model prompts and replies stay local. With a remote server, you decide exactly where your data goes.

Language support

The app can translate menus, settings, onboarding, and app-generated error messages using on-device ML Kit translation after the selected language model is downloaded. Translations are machine generated and may not always be accurate, so use care with important or safety-critical information.

Great for

- Developers testing and comparing AI models
- Researchers experimenting with LLMs
- Anyone who wants private, offline-capable AI on their phone

Requirements

- Android 7.0 (API 24) or higher
- For remote chat: an accessible Ollama server
- For on-device chat: free storage for the model you choose
